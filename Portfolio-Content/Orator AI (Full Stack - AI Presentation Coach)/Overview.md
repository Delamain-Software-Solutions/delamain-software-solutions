Orator AI is an AI powered presentation coach that scores how you present. Users record in the browser or upload a video, then get a clear breakdown of their verbal and non verbal communication.

The app is built with a React frontend and a Django REST Framework backend. It uses MediaPipe for computer vision and custom machine learning models to read gestures, posture, and body language, then turns all of that into clear analytics and a score.

Main features: Gesture and Posture analysis, speech and delivery scoring, and a detailed results dashboard.
